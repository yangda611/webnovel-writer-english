# 第0001章-Ferry Cost

The ferry's menu offered Tamsin two ways to cross.

Passenger: 3 coins. Cargo: 1 coin per sealed crate. No living cargo.

She had two coins, a crate of pears, and a fever. On the far bank, the clinic's blue flag was still flying. It came down at sunset.

“Don't try the crate,” the ferryman said. “It checks.”

Tamsin shut the lid she had just lifted. Her interface confirmed the rule when she touched it. The fare was one thing; the penalty for concealing a passenger was another. She would lose access to every registered crossing for seven days.

She tipped the pears onto the quay.

The ferryman watched them roll. “The crate isn't worth a coin.”

“It has to be sealed, doesn't it?”

He waited.

Tamsin opened her inventory. The medicine token belonged to her mother; she had carried it since the winter cough. It could pay for one treatment, anywhere the blue flag flew. It could not pay a ferry fare.

She put it inside the empty crate and closed the lid.

“Deliver that to the clinic. Ask them to send someone back.”

The menu lit green. One coin vanished from her balance. The ferry pulled away without her.

Tamsin sat on the quay and picked up a pear. She had found a legal move. Now she had to trust a stranger to make the next one.
