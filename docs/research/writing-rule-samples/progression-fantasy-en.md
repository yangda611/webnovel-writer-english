# 第0001章-The Second Note

The bell rang once. The second note died inside the tower.

Below, the fishing boats kept their lights turned toward the harbor. Two notes meant the channel was clear. One meant wait. In another hour the tide would leave them beyond the reef until morning, and the smallest boat had already shipped enough water to put its lamp near the surface.

Sera lifted her hands again. Her teacher caught her wrist.

“Listen before you pull.”

She had spent the afternoon learning to carry a note through the bronze. More force made it louder. It also shook the old crack beneath the rim. The bell swallowed every echo she tried to send after it.

Sera laid two fingers against the metal. The crack trembled after the sound had gone. Not silence, then. Something left over.

She tried humming with it. Nothing happened. She lowered her voice, missed, and felt the vibration tug against her fingertips. On the next breath she found the pitch.

The tower answered with a thin second note.

Her teacher released her wrist. “Again.”

It took four attempts. By the fourth, Sera could hear the right pitch before she touched the bell. She gave the channel signal, two clear notes, and the nearest boat turned toward the harbor mouth.

The lamp on the little boat remained still. Sera leaned out far enough to see its mast against the water. The sail was down. They had heard her; they could not move.

She took her coat from the hook. The new note was hers now. It had bought her time to reach the rescue skiff, nothing more.
