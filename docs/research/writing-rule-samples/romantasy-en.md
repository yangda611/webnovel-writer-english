# 第0001章-The Unbound Gate

Noa offered Evan the knife hilt first.

The oath between them shone across her palm, a narrow thread that had kept the eastern gate closed for eleven years. Cutting it would free him to leave. It would also leave the gate without a keeper.

“The wardens will call it desertion,” he said.

“They can take that up with me.”

He looked toward the window. Beyond the glass, children were helping carry water to the gatehouse. He had taught half of them to tie a bucket knot. Noa had expected anger, perhaps relief. She had forgotten how much of a life he had made inside the boundary she had sworn to enforce.

“You should have asked me,” he said.

“Eleven years ago?”

“This morning. Before you decided that leaving was what I wanted.”

Her fingers tightened around the blade. She turned it back, keeping the edge away from them both, and laid it on the table.

“What do you want?”

“To take a watch because I choose to. To visit my sister without your permission.” He rested a hand beside the knife. “And to know whether you'd still have supper with me if you weren't responsible for where I went afterward.”

“I would.”

“Then ask me tomorrow.”

Noa moved her hand away from the oath. Evan took the knife. When the thread broke, the gate bell rang once, calling for a keeper. He let it ring a second time before he went to answer.
